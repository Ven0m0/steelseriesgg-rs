* **Algorithmic Complexity vs Benchmark Reality:** When optimizing very small collections (like <= 15 items in `Apex2023DirectCommand`), standard `O(N)` slice iterations (`for` loops) are frequently faster than complex `O(1)` array maps or hashing due to CPU cache locality and lack of bounds-checking/hashing overhead.
* **SmallVec for Allocation Optimization:** To eliminate heap allocations while maintaining compatibility with `Vec` APIs and `serde` sequences, use `smallvec::SmallVec<[Type; MAX_CAPACITY]>`. Remember to enable the `serde` feature flag in `Cargo.toml` if the struct derives serialization traits.
* **Serde Skips and Defaults:** Avoid adding skipped arrays (`#[serde(skip, default)] index_map: [u8; 256]`) to public structs that derive `Deserialize` unless the deserialization process explicitly reconstructs the index map. Otherwise, it causes severe desynchronization between internal arrays and vectors during instantiation.
